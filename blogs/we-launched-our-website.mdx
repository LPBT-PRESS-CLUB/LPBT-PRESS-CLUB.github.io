After months of drafts passed around in chat threads, the **LPBT Press Club** now has a permanent
home on the web. It is set in ink, printed on newsprint-coloured pixels, and published straight
from our repository every time someone files a story.

## What's on the page

The front page carries everything a reader needs to find their way around the newsroom:

- **The Newsroom** — who is on staff, which desks they sit at, and what they cover.
- **Desks & Colour Codes** — every community we run, each with its own colour.
- **Back Issues** — the events we have already held, with a write-up of each.
- **Dispatches** — posts like this one, written by members and filed straight to the site.
- **Letters to the Editor** — an anonymous letters box, open to anyone.

<PullQuote>A newsroom is only as good as the letters it gets back.</PullQuote>

## How dispatches get filed

Every dispatch is a single MDX file in the `blogs/` folder. When the site is deployed, the press
scans that folder, typesets each file into its own page, and adds it to the front page — newest
first, tagged with the desk that filed it. No one has to touch the homepage by hand.

## Write to us

If something on the site is wrong, missing, or simply ugly, tell us. The
[letters box](https://www.admonymous.co/not-so-lain) is anonymous, and every letter is read.
