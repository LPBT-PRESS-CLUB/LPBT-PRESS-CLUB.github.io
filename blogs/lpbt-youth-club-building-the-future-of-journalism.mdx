The Lycée Pilote Bourguiba Tunis Youth Club (LPBT YC) is a student-led club driven by creativity, communication, and a passion for journalism. It brings together young people who want to learn, write, question, and share stories that matter to their communities.

At a time when young voices are more important than ever, LPBT YC offers a space for students to explore journalism, develop their communication skills, and contribute fresh perspectives to the world of media.

## A newsroom built by students

The club is not only about publishing stories. It is also about learning how to report responsibly, frame ideas clearly, and work as a team. Through regular activities and collaborative projects, members gain experience in writing, discussion, and media engagement while building confidence in their own voice.

That culture of participation is central to the club's identity. Journalism becomes more than a subject or a hobby; it becomes a practice that encourages curiosity, critical thinking, and public engagement.

## Why it matters

Young people often have the strongest instinct for what is happening around them, but they do not always have the space or platform to express it. LPBT YC creates that space.

By encouraging students to share ideas and tell local stories, the club helps turn attention into action. It strengthens communication within the school community and helps build a culture in which young people see themselves as contributors to public life, not only as consumers of it.

<PullQuote>Young voices deserve a platform, and journalism gives them one.</PullQuote>

## Connecting with the wider public

The club also maintains an active presence on Instagram, where it shares its activities and keeps its audience informed about what it is doing and what it stands for.

Follow LPBT Youth Club on Instagram: https://www.instagram.com/lpbtyc/

This online presence helps extend the club's reach beyond school walls and gives people a simple way to follow its work, support its projects, and discover the ideas it is promoting.

## Looking forward

With passion, teamwork, and a commitment to journalistic excellence, LPBT YC continues to work toward becoming a leading student journalism club in Tunis. Its ambition is clear: to create a welcoming place where young people can learn, write, and contribute to the public conversation with confidence and purpose.

The future of journalism is not shaped only by institutions or professionals. It is also shaped by students who are ready to ask better questions, tell stronger stories, and build a more informed community.
