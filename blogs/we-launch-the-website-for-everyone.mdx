On 1 October 2026, the **LPBT Press Club** officially opens its website to the public. After months of work, experiments, drafts, and late-night edits, the newsroom now has a home that is open for everyone to read, browse, and return to.

The site is designed as a digital broadsheet: a place for notices, features, desk updates, event reports, and the continuing work of the club itself. It brings together the newsroom, the desk structure, the archive of past events, and the growing archive of dispatches in one place.

## What the website offers

Readers can now find:

- **The Newsroom** — who is behind the club, what each member does, and which desks they represent.
- **Desks & Colour Codes** — the communities and interests that make up the Press Club.
- **Back Issues** — the record of events and initiatives already held by the club.
- **Dispatches** — written pieces, short features, and updates from inside the newsroom.
- **Letters to the Editor** — an anonymous channel for feedback, criticism, and ideas.

<PullQuote>We are not building a private archive; we are building a public record.</PullQuote>

## Why this matters

A website is more than a front page. It is a way to share what the club is doing, make our work easier to find, and give the wider community a clear way to follow the story of LPBT Press Club as it develops.

From the first founding meeting to the first public launch, this project has been shaped by the idea that the club should be visible and legible to the people it serves. The launch is a step toward that goal.

We are opening the doors, publishing the work, and inviting everyone in.

## A public beginning

This is not the end of the project, but the start of a new phase. The site will continue to grow with each event, each story, and each edition that comes out of the newsroom.

We welcome readers, members, and anyone curious enough to stop by.
