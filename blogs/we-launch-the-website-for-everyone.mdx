As of today, 1 October 2026, the **LPBT Press Club** has a public home on the web. Our drafts,
plans, and late-night edits used to live in chat threads only members could see. Now they have a
front page anyone can open, read, and come back to.

We built it to look like a broadsheet because that is how we think of ourselves: a newsroom that
puts its work out in public. It sits under our name and gets updated every time someone files a
story.

## What you'll find

- **The Newsroom.** Who runs the club, what each person does, and which desk they sit at.
- **Desks.** The communities that make up the club, from the Newsdesk to Arts & Culture, each with
  its own colour.
- **Back Issues.** A record of the events we have held, with a short write-up of each.
- **Dispatches.** Posts like this one, written by members and published straight to the site.
- **Letters to the Editor.** An anonymous box for feedback, corrections, and ideas.

<PullQuote>We are not building a private archive; we are building a public record.</PullQuote>

## Why it matters

A club that only talks to itself is hard to join and easy to forget. Having a website gives people
one link to see what we do, who does it, and how to get involved. It also keeps us accountable:
once something is printed on the front page, everyone can see whether we followed through.

## What comes next

This is the first edition, not the finished paper. New dispatches, events, and desks will be added
as the club grows, and the site will change with them.

If you spot something wrong, missing, or badly set, tell us. The
[letters box](https://www.admonymous.co/not-so-lain) is anonymous, and every letter gets read.
If you would rather write for us than to us, find a desk that suits you on the front page and get
in touch.
