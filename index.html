From the Editor’s Desk

Press Club Opens Its Doors,
Promises Nothing But Words

A loose federation of writers, coders and insomniacs has declared itself a newsroom. No printing press was harmed in the process.

By The Standing Committee · LPBT Newsroom

The LPBT Press Club is a volunteer newsroom: a place where members of our communities write, edit, illustrate and argue in public. There is no advertising department, no shareholder call, and no editorial line beyond be interesting and be honest.

Work is organised into desks — each with its own colour, its own temperament, and its own standards for what counts as a story. Members may sit at as many desks as they can carry. The people currently holding the pens are listed in The Newsroom; everything we have already published is filed in Back Issues.

Corrections, compliments and unhinged complaints are all welcome, and all anonymous. The letters box does not record who you are — which has historically improved the quality of the letters.

To the Editor,

We accept anonymous letters, tips, corrections and grievances. Nothing you send is linked to your name, your account, or your reputation — we receive the words and nothing else.

Useful things to send us: a story we got wrong, a story we missed, a desk you want opened, or a sentence you think we should be ashamed of.

— Management
